# StudyQuest

## What it does
StudyQuest is a homework planner that turns schoolwork into a game.

## Five important features
1. **Add and edit assignments:** Students add homework with a name, subject, due date and
   difficulty, and can change any of it later.
2. **Dashboard:** The home screen shows what's due soon, what's overdue, and the student's
   XP, level and streak.
3. **Filter and sort:** Students can show only one subject's assignments and sort them by
   due date to see what to do first.
4. **Complete, edit and delete:** Students check off finished assignments, fix mistakes,
   and remove assignments they don't need.
5. 5. **Quest rewards:** Every finished assignment earns XP and fills today's progress bar.
   When everything due tomorrow is done, the bar fills up and the student reaches the day's
   checkpoint. Enough XP raises your level, finishing work days in a row builds a streak,
   and special achievements unlock badges.
